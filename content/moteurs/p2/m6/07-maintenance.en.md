The induction motor is rugged, but it does not last for ever. Its enemies: **heat**, **moisture**, **dust** and **vibration**.

## Preventive maintenance

- **Clean** the cooling fins and the fan cover: a dirty motor cools badly.
- **Listen and feel** (with the back of the hand, motor stopped): a bearing noise or abnormal heating announce a failure.
- **Grease** the bearings that need it, with the grease and quantity indicated: too much grease causes heating.
- **Retighten** the connections and check the cable gland.
- Check **vibration** and alignment.

## Checking the windings

Motor **locked out and disconnected**, links removed, measure the resistance of each winding with the ohmmeter: U1-U2, V1-V2, W1-W2.

![The three windings U1-U2, V1-V2, W1-W2 of a disconnected motor must show the same resistance, here about 2.7 Ω](/static/content/moteurs/en/m6/resistances.svg)

The three values must be **equal within a few %**. A clearly lower value signals shorted turns; an infinite value, an open winding. The **insulation** of the windings to the frame is measured with a special instrument at high voltage (insulation tester): this is work reserved for an authorised technician, never done in this course.

## Troubleshooting a motor that will not start

Work from the simplest to the most complex, from the supply towards the motor:

![Troubleshooting steps: voltage at the terminals, protection tripped, winding resistances, insulation and bearings](/static/content/moteurs/en/m6/diagnostic.svg)

1. Does the voltage reach the terminals, on all **three** phases? A motor with only two phases does not start and hums: this is **single phasing**, which destroys it in minutes if the protection does not trip.
2. Has the protection tripped? Why (overload, stall, wrong setting)?
3. Are the windings sound (equal resistances)?
4. Does the shaft turn freely by hand? If not: bearings or driven machine.

## Key points

- Heat, moisture, dust, vibration: the four enemies.
- Three winding resistances equal within a few %.
- Running on two phases: the motor hums and burns; the protection must trip.
