**Manual control devices** turn an operator's gesture into an electrical order; **indicators** report the machine's state back. Their colours are not free: the standard **IEC 60204-1** sets them, so that an operator finds their way on any machine.

![Button and indicator colours: red for emergency stop, white preferred for start, black for stop; red, yellow, green and blue indicators](/static/content/moteurs/en/m4/couleurs.svg)

## Push buttons

- **Red**: reserved for the **emergency stop** (mushroom head, latching, on a yellow background).
- **White** (preferred), grey or black: **start**; green is still allowed.
- **Black**, grey or white: normal **stop**.
- **Yellow**: intervention in an abnormal situation.

A stop button is wired with a **break** contact: if a wire breaks, the machine stops instead of becoming impossible to stop. This is a general safety rule: stop orders go through break contacts.

## Indicators

Red for danger or a fault, yellow for an abnormal situation, green for normal operation, blue for a mandatory action, white for neutral information.

## Selector switches

A **selector switch** keeps its position. A three-position "manual, 0, auto" selector chooses, for example, how a pump runs. Its **contact table** shows which contact is closed in each position.

![Manual, 0, auto selector: 13-14 closed in manual, 23-24 closed in auto, nothing in 0](/static/content/moteurs/en/m4/selecteur.svg)

## Key points

- Red = emergency stop; white preferred for start.
- Stop always goes through a break contact.
- A selector's contact table is read position by position.
