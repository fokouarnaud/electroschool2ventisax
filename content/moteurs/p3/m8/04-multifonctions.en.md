Rather than stocking one relay per function, **multifunction timer relays** are used: a selector chooses the function, a dial sets the time.

![Multifunction relay: a selector chooses the function (on-delay, off-delay, pulse, flasher), a dial sets the time](/static/content/moteurs/en/m8/fonctions.svg)

Common functions:

- **on-delay** and **off-delay** (previous section);
- **pulse**: the contact closes as soon as the control comes on and opens after the delay, even if the control lasts; useful to send a pulse of fixed length;
- **flasher**: as long as the control is present, the contact alternates closed and open.

![Flasher function: as long as the control is present, the contact alternates on and off](/static/content/moteurs/en/m8/clignotant.svg)

**Example.** A fault indicator flashes 1.5 s on and 1.5 s off: the period is 3 s, that is 20 flashes per minute. A flashing indicator catches the eye much better than a steady one.

## Read the instructions

Each manufacturer designates the functions with its own letters or pictograms: **always** read the relay's instructions (its timing chart) before choosing a selector position. The timing chart, like those in this module, shows the control state and the contact state on the same time scale.

## Key points

- A selector for the function, a dial for the time.
- Pulse: fixed length whatever the control.
- Always read the timing chart in the instructions.
