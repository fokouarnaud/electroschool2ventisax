This mini-project is done in the workshop, with your trainer. Bring your completed measurement table.

## Goal

Identify a transistor's leads, test it, then measure its gain β.

## Parts

| Quantity | Component |
|---|---|
| 1 | BC547 NPN transistor (or 2N2222) |
| 1 | breadboard and wires |
| 1 | 9 V battery and its clip |
| 2 | resistors: 100 kΩ (base), 1 kΩ (collector) |
| 2 | multimeters (or one, used twice) |

## Steps

1. Find the E, B, C leads in your model's datasheet.
2. Test the transistor in diode mode: write down the six readings (three pairs of leads, two directions).
3. Build the bias circuit: 9 V through RB = 100 kΩ to the base, RC = 1 kΩ in the collector, emitter to ground.
4. Calculate the expected IB, then measure IB (ammeter in the base, µA range) and IC (in the collector, mA range).
5. Work out β = IC / IB. Compare it with the datasheet range.
6. Measure VCE. Is the transistor in the linear region or saturated?
