This mini-project is done in the workshop, with your trainer. It is not marked online: bring your completed measurement table.

## Goal

Check by measurement that U = R × I, then test a voltage divider.

## Parts

| Quantity | Component |
|---|---|
| 1 | breadboard and jumper wires |
| 1 | 9 V battery and its clip |
| 3 | resistors: 1 kΩ, 2.2 kΩ, 4.7 kΩ (1/4 W) |
| 1 | digital multimeter |

## Steps

1. Read each resistor's colour code, then measure it with the ohmmeter, **out of circuit**. Note the difference.
2. Build a loop with the battery and the 1 kΩ resistor. Measure the voltage across it (voltmeter in parallel), then the current (ammeter in series, mA range).
3. Repeat with 2.2 kΩ and 4.7 kΩ.
4. For each row, work out U / I and compare it with the ohmmeter reading.
5. Build a divider with 4.7 kΩ at the top and 2.2 kΩ at the bottom. Calculate Vout, then measure it.

## Safety

Never connect the ammeter directly across the battery. Disconnect the battery before changing the circuit.
